Welcome to my website!

I'm James, a third-year PhD student in MIT EECS advised by Priya Donti. My research explores how to overcome the scarcity of real-world grid data as we seek to translate the successes of modern machine learning to power grid applications. Questions I think about include:
- How can we leverage our understanding of grid physics to make the most of the data we do have?
- How can we develop models that effectively use imperfect, multimodal data?
- How can grid operators and stakeholders make more data available without compromising security or privacy?

<div style="overflow: hidden" markdown="1">
I received my SM in EECS from MIT in May 2026, where I was fortunate to be co-advised by Ali Jadbabaie. I graduated from Caltech in June 2024 with a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering. I worked on enforcing real-world constraints in learning-augmented online optimization with Adam Wierman, as well as non-asymptotic information theory with Victoria Kostina.
